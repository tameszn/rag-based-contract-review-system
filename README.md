# rag-based-contract-review-system
  A demo project which behaves as a contract intelligense and review system.Reviewing lengthy legal contracts manually is time-consuming and prone to human oversight. This system automates contract analysis by parsing PDF contracts along their natural clause boundaries and evaluating them across four critical legal pillars:
  * Compliance & Legal Issues: Regulatory requirements, governing laws, and mandatory notifications.
  * Liability Issues: Liability caps, indemnification obligations, and risk allocation.
  * Financial Issues: Payment terms, hidden costs, and financial penalties.
  * Termination & IP Issues: Exit conditions, breach triggers, and intellectual property rights.

## TECH STACK:
* python
* langchain
* google gemini API
* chromaDB
